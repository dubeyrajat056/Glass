About Taiton Hardware

Our Story

Decades of Excellence in Architectural Hardware

Taiton Hardware has been at the forefront of architectural glass hardware manufacturing in India since 1995. Our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction has made us a trusted name in the industry.

With over 50 branch stores and 700+ active members across India, we continue to shape the future of architectural hardware with cutting-edge designs and premium materials. Our products are exported to 15+ countries and used in prestigious commercial and residential projects worldwide.
